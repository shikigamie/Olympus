⚙ Settings · Game paused

Bars: red = HP, blue = MP (skills cost MP), green = EXP.
Skills: click the skill buttons (or keys 1-3). The ⚡ button toggles auto mode: green = on, black = off.
Auto Equip & Sell: drops better than your gear are equipped, worse ones are sold. Store purchases are never automatic.
Rarity: gray Common, green Uncommon, blue Rare, purple Epic, orange Legendary, pink Mythic.
Gold: from kills and selling. Spend it in the Store.
Pets: buy in the Store, equip up to 2, upgrade to Lv 5. They support only.
Stats: STR = Knight ATK, INT = Mage ATK + skill power + MP, DEX = Archer ATK + crit, AGI = attack speed, VIT = HP and DEF.
Road to Hades: bosses guard Lv 5, 10, 15 and 20. If you are defeated you lose 5 levels (stats are kept).
Potions: HP and MP potions (keys Q and W) have a 5s cooldown per type (HP potion only 2s in Endless). The bottle button opens buff elixirs (45s). Buy them in the Store. Auto HP/MP Potion and Auto Buff Potion are in Settings (buffs are used automatically during fights).
Equipment traits: rare gear can carry ★ Stun (a chance on each hit to freeze a foe for a few seconds, bosses resist) or ★ Dodge (a chance to avoid a hit). Store gear always has one.
Endless: after Lord Hades the Endless begins with all-new, much stronger monsters (x2.5 damage, tougher armor, stronger self-buffs). Levels continue to 30 with guardians at Lv 25 and Lv 30.
Zones: the area changes every 5 levels and has an effect (shown on the HUD): Garden = faster HP regen but foes heal, Harbor = faster attacks but foes hit harder, Underworld = burning damage (Legendary+ gear and the Nymph pet resist it).
Quests: new daily quests every day. Claim the gold when done.
